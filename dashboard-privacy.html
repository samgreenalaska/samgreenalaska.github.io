Privacy

Privacy policy for Sam's Dashboard

Sam's Dashboard is a private website for Sam Green, his friends, and his family. Only people he approves can sign in.

What it receives

Sign-in uses Google and requests the openid and email scopes. Google returns your email address, confirmation that it is verified, and an account ID. The application does not request your name, photo, contacts, mail, or files.

How it is used

Your email address is checked against the list of approved people. That check decides whether you can sign in and which pages you can open. The address is shown on the page while you are signed in.

What is stored

A server Sam operates on his own cloud instance stores the list of approved email addresses. For each sign-in it stores your email address and the times the session started and was last used. Server logs record sign-in attempts with the email address, including attempts from accounts that are not approved. The account ID is not stored.

Sharing

This data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Cookies

The dashboard sets two cookies. One keeps you signed in. The other protects the sign-in process and lasts 10 minutes. Neither tracks you.

Analytics

This page and the dashboard carry no analytics. Other pages on this site use PostHog to count visits.

Revoking access

Signing out deletes your session record. You can remove the application from the Google Account permissions page. To have your email address removed from the approved list, use the contact below.

Limited use

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to samgreenalaska@gmail.com.
