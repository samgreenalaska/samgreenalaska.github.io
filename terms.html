Terms of service for gmail-mcp

gmail-mcp is private software written and run by Sam Green for his own Gmail accounts. It is not a public service and it accepts no other users.

Who may use it

Only the operator. The application authorizes four Gmail accounts, all his own. There is no sign-up, no account creation, and no way for another person to connect a mailbox to it.

What it does

It exposes Gmail to an AI assistant through the Model Context Protocol, so mail can be searched, read, labelled and drafted from a chat interface. Full detail on what data is touched is in the privacy policy.

Availability

The service runs on a single small cloud instance and may be stopped, changed or removed at any time without notice. No uptime is promised.
